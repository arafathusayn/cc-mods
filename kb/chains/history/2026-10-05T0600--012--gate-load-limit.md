# The check gate's load limit from the environment

`978e77a` made the load gate's limit `CC_MODS_GATE_LOAD_PERCENT` percent of the cores (60 when
unset or empty), decoded before the lock is taken, and made each wait print the limits it is held
to. ADR-0015 supersedes ADR-0009.

## Evidence

- Unit tests: 14 cores give a limit of 8 at the default, 11 at 80, 21 at 150 and 1 at 1; the
  wait line reads `waiting for room: load 12.00, 40% memory free (needs load under 6, 20% memory
  free)` for 10 cores.
- `CC_MODS_GATE_LOAD_PERCENT` set to `0`, `abc`, `2.5` or `-5` → `✘ CC_MODS_GATE_LOAD_PERCENT: $
  must be a positive integer (got string)`, exit 2.
- `bun run check` → `✔ 6 checks passed`.
